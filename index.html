Wistful Youth (2016)

Title: Wistful Youth (2016) A short film made by a 14-year-old, about the confusion, choices, and quiet chaos of growing up. Description: In the summer of 2016, when I was in 8th grade, I spent a week at a friend’s house filming Wistful Youth — my first attempt at writing and directing a short film. It’s a story about being young and lost: the uncertainty of who you want to be, the fragile weight of friendships, and that strange in-between space where childhood is fading, but nothing feels clear yet. This 44-minute version is the full cut I edited myself. I also made a 20-minute version for a school competition — somehow, it won first prize. I still have the little trophy. That summer was probably the wildest and most unforgettable part of my youth. We knocked on strangers’ doors looking for a foreigner to play a role, scrambled to recast actors after sudden fights, and filmed scenes with whatever props and chaos we could find. It was messy, hilarious, and painfully sincere. More than a decade years later, I’ve lost touch with nearly everyone from that summer. But this film — clumsy as it is — still carries the echoes of who we were.

Bitter Youth (2019)

"Bitter Youth" (2019) is a short film created by a talented group of Chinese high school students, all around 16 years old. This special student group wrote and shot the film, which delves into the themes of friendship, romance, and depression, presenting a realistic portrayal of adolescent life in China. However, the student directing group faced significant challenges. Due to its depiction of teenage depression, the film was banned by their school. Despite this setback, we are thrilled to finally share this remarkable work with you, five years later.
